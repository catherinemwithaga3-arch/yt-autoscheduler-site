Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

YT Shorts Autoscheduler ("the app") is a personal automation tool used only by its owner to manage their own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and publish time on the owner's channel.
  • YouTube Analytics: read-only access to the owner's channel performance metrics (views, watch time, retention, engagement).
  • Google Drive: read-only access to video files the owner places in a designated folder.
  • Google Sheets: read and write access to the owner's scheduling sheet, to read the queue and record upload status and statistics.

How data is used

Data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and report their performance to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage

Video files are downloaded temporarily during upload and deleted immediately afterwards. Access credentials are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to run the app.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
