YT Shorts Autoscheduler

A private tool used by its owner to upload and schedule videos to their own YouTube channel automatically, using the YouTube Data API.

It reads a queue of videos from the owner's Google Drive and Google Sheets, uploads each video to YouTube as private with a scheduled publish time, and records the result back in the sheet.

This app is not offered to the public and only accesses the owner's own accounts.
